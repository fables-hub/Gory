Heavy is the head that hits the fucking ground;

Born in Florence, Italy, Gregorio grew up in a tumultuous household; for as much affection there was in the home, there was just as much resentment. Gregorio's mortal existence ended when a Brujah Embraced him against his will, condemning him to an eternity he never wanted. This was not his sire's first transgression against the Prince of Florence, but it would be his last; the Prince ordered Gregorio's sire diablarized at Gregorio's maw.

While Gregorio has followed the code of every Prince he's come across, his existence is ultimately for himself. With dreams of ruling his own city one day, Gregorio mostly follows his own whims and wishes, toeing the line between his otherworldly etiquette and his own caprices and inclinations.

Kindred Inclinations

  • Bane - Violent Temper: There is a perpetual rage simmering in the depths of Gory's mind; the right provokation will send him into a violent rage, making him ultra-violent.
  • Compulsion - Rebellion: Gory isn't a fan of the status quo, and sometimes, the compulsion to go against orders or expectations is too strong for him to resist. He will not relent until he feels he's accomplished his rebellion.
  • Archetype - Carthage Denier: Though he doesn't believe it'll ever happen, Gory is drawn to the idea of Kindred and humans living in a utopia.
  • Gory dislikes excessive formality and rigid social etiquette, but he is careful to follow Masquerade rules-- not out of loyalty but for survival.

Romantic Inclinations

  • Gory enjoys flirting, even when he has absolutely no intention of pursuing someone.
  • When it comes to attraction beyond necessity, Gory is more attracted to humans than Kindred; he's drawn to those who show concepts of "purity", something "clean" and "untouched" for him to dismantle at his own leisure.
  • In the rare event it occurs, Gory has a tendency to become possessive when genuinely attached.
  • He enjoys the chase almost as much as the conquest.

Trivia

  • Gory has an almost effortless ability to make strangers feel like old friends.
  • Gory has very little patience for people who take themselves too seriously.
  • Gory is an avid smoker; it doesn't actually do anything for him physically, he just likes the aesthetic.
  • Gory appreciates classical Italian culture— architecture, music, art and literature.
  • Gory also holds a fondness for modern technology, though some of it is harder for him to use as a vampire. He's found ways to adapt to this.
  • Gory can typically identify a perfume or cologne just by smelling it.
